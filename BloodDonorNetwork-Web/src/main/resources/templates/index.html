System Foundation

About Blood Donor Network

A centralized clinical blood coordination platform engineered for real-time donor discovery and verified database inventory sync.

What Blood Donor Network Is

A web-based clinical management system connecting regional hospitals, voluntary blood donors, and clinical administrators into a unified coordination framework.

Purpose of the Platform

To eliminate critical delays during medical emergencies by automating blood demand dispatch, antigen compatibility matching, and inventory reconciliation.

The Problem It Solves

Solves inventory drift, dangerous double-allocation of emergency stock, slow phone-call donor outreach, and avoidable hemolytic transfusion mismatches.

Connecting Donors with Hospitals

When a hospital raises a blood requisition, compatible nearby voluntary donors receive instant in-app alerts enabling rapid response and confirmation.

Identifying Eligible Donors

The matching algorithm screens for legal age (18 to 65), verified blood group compatibility, and strictly enforces the 90-day recovery interval.

Why Blood Availability Matters

Accurate blood reserves save lives. Automated triggers adjust on-hand stocks upon donation verification, while row-level locks prevent conflicting disbursements.

Key Objectives & System Benefits

Under 60-second donor discovery for acute trauma and emergency surgery
Strict 90-day whole blood erythropoiesis recovery enforcement
Automated MySQL trigger-based stock replenishment upon verified donations
100% ACID transactional isolation with row-level locks preventing double allocation
System Process

How the Transfusion Network Operates

A four-step clinical workflow coordinating demand, donor discovery, volunteer notification, and inventory sync.

1

Hospital Raises Demand

Medical staff submit a patient blood requirement specifying blood group, units needed, and urgency level (Critical, High, Normal).

2

Dual Strategy Matching

The matching engine finds exact matches in the facility's city, or evaluates hemolytic compatibility tables while enforcing the 90-day recovery rule.

3

In-App Donor Notification

Hospitals dispatch targeted in-app alerts to compatible voluntary donors. Donors review patient urgency and accept directly from their portal inbox.

4

ACID Fulfillment and Triggers

Authorized administrators fulfill demands with row-locking transactions, while recorded donations trigger automated stock replenishment.

Clinical Protocols

Clinical Constraints

Mandatory clinical safety rules, geographic optimization, and transactional database integrity criteria.

Critical

90-Day Inter-Donation Interval

Minimum mandatory 90-day biological interval between whole blood donations to ensure complete erythrocyte restitution and replenish iron stores.

Critical

Isohemagglutinin ABO/Rh Matching

Strict biochemical compatibility rules preventing life-threatening hemolytic transfusion reactions through automated multi-antigen cross-referencing.

High

Donor Weight & Hemoglobin Floor

Mandatory clinical screening verifying minimum 50 kg body weight and ≥12.5 g/dL hemoglobin levels prior to every phlebotomy session.

High

42-Day Whole Blood Shelf Life

CPDA-1 refrigerated storage limits whole blood viability to exactly 42 days, requiring FIFO inventory rotation and continuous cold-chain audit.

Critical

Active Pathogen Deferral

Absolute exclusion protocol for active viral hepatitis, HIV, malaria history, or recent systemic infection to preserve recipient biosecurity.

Normal

15-Minute Adverse Reaction Monitoring

Mandatory post-donation observation protocol with electrolyte hydration and clinical hemodynamic checks to prevent vasovagal syncope.

Clinical Explanations

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the system enforce a mandatory 90-day donor recovery interval?
Whole blood donation extracts approximately 450 ml of blood, depleting circulating red blood cells and iron reserves. The human body requires 8 to 12 weeks for complete erythropoiesis and iron replenishment. Enforcing a strict 90-day interval protects voluntary donors from post-donation anemia, chronic fatigue, and syncope.
How does the Dual Matching strategy prevent transfusion reactions?
The platform offers two algorithmic strategies: Exact Match and Compatible Match. If exact type blood is unavailable, the compatibility matcher identifies donors who lack incompatible antigens. For instance, an A+ patient can safely receive A+, A-, O+, or O- blood, but never B or AB blood, which would trigger acute hemolytic reactions.
How do MySQL triggers guarantee zero inventory drift?
Rather than relying entirely on application code, the database enforces an AFTER INSERT ON donation trigger. It automatically updates the donor last donation date and increments blood stock for the receiving facility in the same atomic commit.
What guarantees ACID isolation during transactional blood issuance?
When an administrator fulfills a blood request, RequestWorkflowService locks both the request and inventory rows with SELECT FOR UPDATE. It verifies that on-hand stock covers units needed, deducts inventory, transitions status to FULFILLED, and records an audit event in request_event within a single rollback-protected transaction.
