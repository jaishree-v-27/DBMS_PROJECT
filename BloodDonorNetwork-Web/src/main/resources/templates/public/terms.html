Notice: This document governs access to the Blood Donor Network platform. It requires review by the responsible deployment authority.

1. System Purpose and Medical Disclaimer

The Blood Donor Network platform is an administrative record-keeping, inventory tracking, and donor-matching coordination software system. It does not provide medical clearance or clinical diagnoses. All actual blood draws, pre-donation hemoglobin screenings, serological screenings, and cross-matching tests must be performed in person by qualified medical personnel at licensed blood collection facilities.

2. Voluntary Participation

Blood donation through this network is strictly voluntary and unpaid. Donors may update their availability status at any time through their profile portal.

3. Accuracy of Clinical Information

Participating healthcare facilities agree to enter truthful patient blood requirements and accurate units needed. In-app notifications sent to voluntary donors represent genuine clinical requests.

4. Account Security and Authorization

Users are responsible for safeguarding login credentials. Account brute-force protection locks accounts for 10 minutes following 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts.
