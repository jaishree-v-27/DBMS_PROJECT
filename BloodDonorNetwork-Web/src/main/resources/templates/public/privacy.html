Legal and Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Notice: This document outlines the clinical data collected and processed by the Blood Donor Network platform. It requires review by the responsible deployment authority.

1. Information We Store

The platform stores records necessary to operate clinical blood management and matching. This includes:

  • Voluntary Donors: Full name, age, blood group, contact telephone number, city location, availability status, and last donation timestamp.
  • Healthcare Facilities: Hospital name, city location, contact telephone number, and authorized user credentials.
  • Blood Demands: Patient reference name, blood group, units requested, clinical urgency classification, and status.
  • Donation Records: Date of collection, participating hospital, donor reference, and whole blood units contributed.

2. How Data is Used

Data is processed strictly for:

  • Enforcing the mandatory 90-day clinical recovery period between donations.
  • Determining ABO and Rh blood group transfusion compatibility.
  • Allowing authorized medical staff to notify compatible voluntary donors in their city.
  • Maintaining ACID-compliant audit logs of inventory deductions.

3. Multi-Tenant Access Isolation

Hospital staff can only inspect, raise, and manage blood demands belonging strictly to their authorized healthcare institution. Patient names and contact details are never exposed to public visitors.

4. Security and Storage

Account passwords are encrypted with BCrypt cryptographic hashing. Sensitive dashboards send no-store HTTP cache headers and all state-modifying requests are validated with session-bound CSRF tokens.
